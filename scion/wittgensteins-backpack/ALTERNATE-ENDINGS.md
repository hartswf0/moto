# Three alternate final beats

All three share beats 348–364: he sets the backpack down, opens a door in it, walks in shrinking, and the door shuts. The ending changes from beat 364. Render any of them with `ENDING=A|B|C python3 -I src/render.py --from 348 --to 384.5 --out ending_X`.

| | clip | final beat | reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** (in the film) | [endings/ending_A.mp4](endings/ending_A.mp4) | The box jigs on the beat, then: *WHEREOF ONE CANNOT SPEAK, THEREOF ONE MUST PACK.* | The man is now the beetle in his own box; nobody can look in. |
| **B** | [endings/ending_B.mp4](endings/ending_B.mp4) | The door reopens and out walks the beetle, carrying the word BEETLE as a placard. | The word was what was in the box all along. |
| **C** | [endings/ending_C.mp4](endings/ending_C.mp4) | The door reopens: the box is empty. *THE BOX MIGHT EVEN BE EMPTY.* returns, then the PACK caption. | It calls back the lineup; the man has dropped out of the language game. |

Builder's preference: **C** is the sharpest callback, and **A** is the calmest ending under the fading track. None of the endings has been reviewed by an independent critic.
