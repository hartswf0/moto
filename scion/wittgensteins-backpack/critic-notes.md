# Critic notes

Each round used a fresh independent agent that had not built the work. Each critic saw only the expected experience and the rendered file, never the builder's self-assessment. It inspected frames extracted with ffmpeg, phone-size (360 px) sheets and audio RMS/onsets. **No critic could hear audio.** Every audio claim below is a measurement. The verdicts are kept as given; the reports are lightly condensed.

- **Strengths:** frame-accurate timing, an intentional paper look, a readable beetle, and the beetle exits last.
